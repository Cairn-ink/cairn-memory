## Goal and decision

Deliver a lightweight reliable memory layer usable through MCP, Hermes and
other harnesses. This small experiment must inform a concrete next correction,
not replace the official benchmark or installed-product acceptance. N4 completed
four paired authored cases but two traces were ambiguous and two bypassed model
selection on small complete maps. Its incorrect premise-change answer remains
incorrect; its broad status question is not reused or rescored.

N5 supports one source window on several cards. N6 will use three fresh larger
histories, explicit decision-and-reason questions and original-premise probes.
It distinguishes missing original evidence from failure to explain evidence
actually delivered. The frozen route remains indexed evidence with bounded
source scan, not default MOC traversal, qualification or automatic relation
inference. No new prompts, model, retrieval engine or scorer policy here.

The initial worker suggestion used six sessions per case. Primary requires
16 instead: extracting several facts from one session is not guaranteed to
produce several cards. Six batches could again yield only six cards and miss
selection entirely. Sixteen distinct sessions make that precondition more
plausible without asserting it is guaranteed. Background messages must carry
specific, coherent information useful in their own right. The original reason,
later premise update and proposal/adoption belong to separated sessions; do
not repeat the original rationale in later messages just to help retrieval.
This increases independent sessions and possible current cards, not necessarily
text volume: N6 has 32 messages per case versus N4's 60. It is a selection-path
diagnostic, not a long-document ingestion stress test. Official long-history
completion still needs its separate matched cohort.
