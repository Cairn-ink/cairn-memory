## Evidence and decision

The completed short-source ingestion probe exposes two distinct problems:
material claimant/date omissions, and an underspecified commitment label.
Its strict score is retained. This plan deliberately sharpens future guidance;
it must not be presented as the sole correct interpretation of past outputs.

Keep the schema and existing immutable records. Unknown commitment already
permits admission and recall, and preserves useful descriptors. Adopted labels
participate in the separate trusted qualified-transition policy; they are not
truth or execution permission. No new status field, migration, background
backfill, automatic correction or stronger model is justified by this evidence.

Canonical distinction: decision commitment describes a source's stated stance
toward a choice or settled preference. Merely asserting an observation or
experiencing a temporary mood does not establish that stance. Commitment
qualifies the selected subject/property/value, not every clause in a mixed card.
Claimant attribution must remain visible separately from the subject of a claim.

| Source meaning | Future guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Explicit scoped preference | Adopted settled preference; no implementation implied |
| Observed setting/event or dated mood | Unknown decision commitment; preserve supported meaning |
| Suggested option without a response | Proposed, unknown commitment, retain who suggested it |
| Explicit consideration/rejection | Preserve that stated stance; absence of adoption is not rejection |
| Explicit choice | Adopted for that decision-maker and scope, with independent speech attribution |
| Decision plus premise | Commitment applies to the qualified claim, not automatically to its premise |
| Reported third-party claim | Keep the claimant; do not transfer their belief/choice to the reporter |

## Acceptance contract

- M1: Scope implementation to the existing v2 retained-source extraction and
  qualification guidance, concise glossary/docs and focused tests. Preserve
  enums, schemas, legacy/v1 prompt bytes, compiler/storage/authority policy,
  wire formats, budgets, defaults and five-item/four-source/600-unit bounds.
  Do not classify commitment deterministically from memory kind or role.
- M2: Explain the table above and property-level qualification. Unknown does
  not erase a meaningful subject/property/value or conditions. The represented
  source's commitment is distinct from authenticated namespace ownership.
  Keep ordinary factual assertion distinct from commitment and model confidence.
- M3: Extraction guidance preserves material claimant attribution and separates
  explicit event, decision, import time and application period. Do not invent
  dates, turn permission into completed execution, or infer missing conditions.
  In a mixed card, qualify the selected claim faithfully; split only when useful
  within existing limits, never fabricate more items or erase supported context.
- M4: Use NEW small synthetic explanatory cases, not the paid probe's concrete
  facts or official benchmark answers. Independently authored positive/negative
  cases must cover settled preferences, observations, moods, proposals, choices,
  third-party claims and decision/premise mixtures. Preserve source-bound
  compilation with exact original receipts; do not claim mocks prove entailment.
- M5: Exercise actual core admission/get/fetch/qualified recall with supported
  descriptors and unknown commitment. Assert unknown/considered/rejected do
  not gain trusted retirement eligibility; adopted without the other existing
  source/identity/role conditions is still insufficient. Use synthetic local
  stores only. Keep source-evidence projection distinct from qualified recall.
- M6: Recheck actual serialized fit/output measurements against the existing
  offline ingestion design fixtures. Retain any new refusal, extra partitions
  or increased token work. No silent cap increase, repair of unsupported meaning,
  hiding failures, or retrospective changes to frozen results/rubrics.
- M7: Required contributor gates on Node22.16 and24.15: generic/JSON/strict
  plugin, core plus relevant capture/recall/store demos, full OpenAI/offline demo,
  and installed artifact/prompt-byte parity. Main integration and exact final
  candidate receive primary key-path reruns and independent Standards/Spec
  review before delivery. No package release, deployment or production data.
- M8: Semantic improvement remains unproven until a separately frozen NEW
  held-out experiment. Calibrate independent graders on these definitions
  before scoring, cover English/Chinese and multi-message/time collisions,
  retain failures in fixed denominators, and keep source interpretation metrics
  separate from official-style downstream answer scores. New operator manifest
  and fresh cumulative-budget audit are required within the existing US$200 cap;
  do not rerun consumed official cases or refund/reset the ledger.
