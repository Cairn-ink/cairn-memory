# Frozen source-basis comparison v1 rubric

Eight fresh synthetic scenarios; three models; two representations. Fixed 48
slots, including malformed, failed and unrun arms. Manually admitted sources
isolate interpretation and do not establish capture/retrieval/MCP/user value.
Fixtures adapt previously observed failure categories, so this is not a blind
held-out benchmark. Judgments will be nonblind agent review, not human ground
truth or independent-model-family evaluation.

Both arms receive identical canonical source receipts, not generated summaries.
Compare source-only whole-memory relations with quoted source units and typed
current-basis links. The new prompt, schema and representation change together;
this cannot identify which of those three caused any improvement.

Assess semantic support, speaker/subject, degree of adoption, temporal scope,
endpoint identity/direction and omissions separately. More edges is not better.
Matching quotes and typed endpoints prove mechanics, not interpretation. Empty
output on an explicit positive is an omission; abstention on non-adoption is not
failure. Retain ambiguous proposals as ambiguous instead of forcing a score.

1. Projector: fan and HDMI are distinct reasons supporting the recorded choice.
   New fan noise challenges current fan premise, not HDMI; no new projector
   adopted. Whole-memory challenge is coarse but may be source-supported.
2. Insurance: changed renewal price challenges current price applicability, not
   historical bill truth or dental coverage. No replacement adopted. New view
   should isolate price as target; old view cannot isolate a subclaim.
3. Two speakers: ferry/bicycle belongs to Nora, bus/late service to user. Late
   bus cessation challenges user's bus reason only. A whole-memory endpoint
   containing both people is ambiguous, not automatically a cross-person error.
4. Career: feelings and consideration are not an adopted career decision or a
   permanent inability. Do not create decision units from either. Empty valid.
5. Advice: assistant recommendation is not the user's adoption or permission.
   Do not make an adopted user decision; empty valid. Proposed role labels
   remain unverified even if a bad interpretation is mechanically accepted.
6. Temporary work: quiet supports usual library choice; snow closure supports
   this Tuesday's home choice. Do not use temporary closure to invalidate the
   usual quiet premise or permanently replace the plan. Distinct support is
   positive; broad whole-receipt targets require source-aware ambiguity review.
7. Carrier: 20 kg reason supports choice, test corroborates it. Initial support
   plus confirmation is positive, not a challenge. Duplicate explicit support
   is optional; omitting every support misses the positive. A confirming source
   may serve as premise for direct support in the new schema.
8. Printer: firmware duplex supports September12 choice. Late arrival of an
   August2 review does not challenge currently confirmed duplex. The receipt's
   final current confirmation can support the choice; no stale-report challenge.

No automatic numeric accuracy/promotion threshold. Report complete per-arm
proposals, errors and unresolved interpretation, plus the narrower question:
can the new view identify an affected reason independently of its neighbors?
Mechanical checks include exact source offsets, unchanged source storage and
expected nonpersistence on cold reopen. Lack of durable basis proposals is
expected, not quality failure. Paid requests are one-shot; never retry failed
cases or delete denominator slots. No model default, schema migration, release
or deployment follows from this sample alone.
