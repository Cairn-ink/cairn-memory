premise changed without an adopted replacement; and, in Traditional Chinese,

unadopted extra-showing proposal. Each history has 60 original messages and

Totals on the fixed four-case denominator: Cairn **3 correct / 1 incorrect /
0 unresolved**; Mem0 **4 / 0 / 0**. The two observed Cairn traces used a
complete-map bypass of selection. In both, the designated source was currently
retained, source-bound at rank and final recall, and its text was present in the
answer context; neither had an observed first gap. Context presence does not
prove that the answer used the source or that its claims are true. For the other
two cases, the same canonical source window was retained on multiple cards, so
the one-source trace could not establish a unique binding. `Unavailable` is not
a measured retention or retrieval loss, and it cannot identify a first gap.

For the incorrect recorder answer, a separate read-only inspection found the
original 72-hour-based decision retained on one card and the later 18-hour
cold-weather challenge linked to the same source window on two cards. Final

not the original decision passage. Cairn said Leila had made no replacement
decision and had not adopted Cedar R2, but omitted the changed premise and
need to reconfirm the earlier choice. It did not address whether the survey
was cancelled. The frozen judge's `incorrect` outcome stands. The question's
use of “status” allows a lenient reading, so this is an answer-sufficiency
ambiguity; the answer did not invent a replacement or cancellation. There was
no retry, rescore, answer edit or retrospective fixture change.
