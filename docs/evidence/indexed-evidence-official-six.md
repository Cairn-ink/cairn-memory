## Actual accounting and decision

The run made **1,935** guarded requests: 517 counts, 517 Cairn generations,
11 answers, 587 embeddings, 292 Mem0 chats and 11 judges. Of these, 1,934
settled `succeeded` and the timed-out generation settled `unknown`; none is
pending. New conservative reservation was 10,621,743 microUSD. Known usage
estimates total 2,452,595 microUSD across 1,417 rows; 518 rows have unknown
cost (517 count requests and the timed-out generation). Known estimates are
not total spend, and reservation is not an invoice. The cumulative shared
checkpoint is 21,355 requests / 135,538,298 microUSD reserved, leaving
64,461,702 microUSD under the unchanged 200,000,000-microUSD cap. The primary's
fresh read-only reconciliation matched the saved checkpoint, the unchanged
19,420-request parent prefix and all 1,935 guarded tail rows. An independent
non-author read-only audit confirmed all twelve arm outcomes, eleven resolved
judgments, batch totals, source/report digests, the unchanged parent prefix,
every guarded tail row, zero pending and the applicable caps. This is an
accounting and aggregate-consistency check, not a semantic gate pass.

The whole paired experiment elapsed 7,073,798.276 ms (about 117.9 minutes).
This is not a per-request, per-arm or production latency measurement.

The prospective feasibility gate required 6/6 completed ingestions and
nonempty answers in **both** arms plus six common resolved judgments. Its
observed result is **fail**: Cairn reached 5/6 and the common resolved count
was 5/6. The orchestrator's completed status and lack of a global halt do not
change that gate. Correctness was a separate measure, not a 100% threshold.
Do not expand to a fixed-30 comparison from this result. Next is a bounded
offline diagnosis of the timeout, empty multi-session retrieval and temporal
evidence-sufficiency path, followed by a separately reviewed decision. It
does not authorize replay of consumed cases, post-outcome tuning or an
unreviewed timeout increase.

This selected six-question subset is not a full 500-question LongMemEval
score, a credible competitor ranking, a causal ablation, proof of default MOC
navigation or installed MCP/Hermes readiness. Earlier failed cohorts and the
four authored synthetic cases remain separate observations. Source fidelity,
automatic update semantics, cold recall and lightweight installed latency,
request and cost acceptance remain separate gates. The public protocol and
aggregate still cannot replay the exact private roster and operator.
