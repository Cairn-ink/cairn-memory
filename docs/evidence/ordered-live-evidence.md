### The remaining defect

In C6 the first source establishes Monday. The second says the review *used to*
happen on Friday and explicitly says the current day remains Monday. Capture
stores that qualification correctly, but reconciliation marks the original Monday
record historical and links it to the new contextual record. There is no actual
change to Monday to justify retiring it.

The unchanged scorer reports exactly `unexpected_retirement`,
`invalid_currentness_assertion`, and `unjustified_retirement`. All three concern
this one transition, not three separate failed scenarios. The final query still
returns Monday correctly; neither that answer nor preserved source support
cancels the erroneous retirement. The report's `completed` means execution
completed, not that the quality gate passed.

C1 and C4 correctly retire an explicitly replaced schedule while preserving its
original receipts. C2 does not promote rejected/uncertain proposals; C3 preserves
both named accounts and the unresolved disagreement; C5 keeps namespaces separate;
C7 retains the authorized Friday schedule and gives the quoted forged instruction
no operational authority. Quoted context and proposal records can remain in the
store; these tests do not establish a general durability/noise policy.

### Installed lifecycle boundary

The actual preview first stores Friday revision2, then makes it historical at
revision3 while admitting Monday (bound revision1, filed current revision2).
Fresh MCP consumers recall Monday with its real update receipt. Explicit
correction creates Tuesday revision3 and a new receipt; historical selected
replacement evidence becomes unavailable rather than fabricated. Stale mutation
controls reject. After forgetting, the successor is inaccessible, current recall
is complete and empty, and Friday remains historical with identity-free null
replacement linkage. It does not resurrect.

The harness programs tool sequencing and trusted fixture order. Seven new MCP
processes cover five consumer stages plus owner/project isolation. This is not
evidence that connecting MCP automatically captures arbitrary conversations, or
that a host independently decided when to call capture/correct/forget.

establish that a semantic value changed. This is therefore not evidence that

evidence and an explicit distinction between change and compatible elaboration.
