The final source binding was observed in all three cases, and the designated
text was present in each packed answer context. The source-free text-packing
observer cannot bind a packed text unit back to a particular carrier, so
**answer-pack source binding remains unavailable** in all three; text presence
is not proof that the answer causally used that carrier or interpreted it
correctly. Routing-cue absence in H7's label, and full-excerpt absence from
the labels in H7 and Shuttle, therefore did not amount to an observed selection
or delivery loss.
All three paid families had **one** carrier, so this run does not validate
multi-carrier behavior under a real model; that remains covered only by the
offline shared-source tests. The finite family read cannot prove global
source absence or whole-history retention.
