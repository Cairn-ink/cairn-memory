# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while examining impact, and criticize ideas rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's private conversation or credentials are not tolerated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact channel on the Cairn.ink website. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
