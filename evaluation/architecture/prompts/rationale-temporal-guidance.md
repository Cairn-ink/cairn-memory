Additional chronology guidance for source-attributed rationale proposals:

Distinguish dates describing an event or claim's applicability from dates of
storage or import; do not substitute an import date for an event date. Array
order is not chronology. An
earlier successful observation remains evidence of that earlier state; by itself
it does not refute an explicit later loss of the same capability. A later
correction may explicitly retract an earlier report, but do not infer a
retraction merely from a newer date.

Keep independently supported reasons for a recorded decision distinct. A
historical reason can remain source-supported even when later evidence
challenges its current applicability. Consider each challenge in the same
subject, property, scope, and applicable conditions, with the direction from
challenging evidence to the challenged premise. Preserve uncertainty when
dates, scope, or reports conflict. Neither a changed premise nor a proposal
establishes a changed decision, present truth, or permission to act. Omit an
edge when its source relationship is not supported; no edge is required.
